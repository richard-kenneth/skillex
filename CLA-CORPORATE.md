**1.1 "Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the Corporation, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

**1.2 "Authorized Contributor"** means an employee, contractor, or agent of the Corporation or its Affiliates who is listed in Schedule A and who is authorized by the Corporation to submit Contributions on the Corporation's behalf.

**1.3 "Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including source code, object code, documentation, tests, designs, graphics, data, configuration, and any modification or addition to an existing work, that an Authorized Contributor intentionally submits to the Project for inclusion in or use by the Project. A Contribution does not include material that the Corporation or the Authorized Contributor clearly identifies in writing as "Not a Contribution" at the time of submission.

**1.4 "Submit"** means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the Project or its representatives, including communication through source-code control systems, issue trackers, pull requests, mailing lists, chat channels, and other collaboration tools. **1.5 "Project"** means the SkillEx open-source project, including its repositories, documentation, websites, applications, and distributions.
