**1.3 "Participating Affiliate"** means an Affiliate whose employees, contractors, or agents are listed in Schedule A.

**1.4 "Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including source code, object code, documentation, tests, designs, graphics, data, configuration, and any modification of or addition to an existing work, that an Authorized Contributor intentionally Submits to the Project for inclusion in or use by the Project. A Contribution does not include material that the Corporation or the Authorized Contributor clearly identifies in writing as "Not a Contribution" at the time of submission.

**1.5 "Submit"** means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the Project or its representatives, including communication through source-code control systems, issue trackers, pull requests, mailing lists, chat channels, and other collaboration tools. **1.6 "Project"** means the SkillEx open-source project, including its repositories, documentation, websites, applications, and distributions.

**1.7 "Project Owner"** means Richard Kenneth and any person or entity to which the Project Owner assigns this Agreement or the rights under it.

**1.8 "Public License"** means the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3, as published by the Free Software Foundation (AGPL-3.0-only).

**1.9 "Writing"** and **"written"** include email, electronic signature, and messages sent through the private channel described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md). A public comment is not written notice.
