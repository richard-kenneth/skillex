# Privacy Notice for Contributors and Community Members

**Version 1.0**

**Effective date: 2 November 2026**

This notice explains how personal information is handled when you sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA), contribute to SkillEx, request a corporate CLA, report a security vulnerability, report a Code of Conduct concern, or send a legal, trademark, or privacy request.

It does not cover the SkillEx product's end users. A separate product privacy policy covers the app and website.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Richard Kenneth**, the owner of the SkillEx project (the "Project Owner", "we", or "us"), based in Nigeria.

**Contact:** **legal@richardkenneth.com**

You do not need a GitHub account to contact us. Do not post identity documents or sensitive personal information in a public issue or discussion.

## What we collect

**Individual CLA and contributions:**

- your GitHub username and GitHub user ID;
- your name and email address, if you enter them in the CLA service or they are prefilled from your GitHub profile;
- the pull request, repository, date, and CLA version associated with your acceptance;
- your confirmation that you are at least 18; and
- correspondence about your contribution or CLA.

Your GitHub username, user ID, and acceptance are **required** to sign the CLA. If you do not provide them, we cannot merge your contribution. Your name and email address are **optional**.

**Corporate CLA:**

- the corporation's legal name, registered address, registration number, and country of incorporation;
- the authorized signatory's name, title, and business contact details;
- the names, GitHub usernames, business email addresses, and affiliations of Authorized Contributors listed in Schedule A;
- the signed agreement and any updates to Schedule A; and
- correspondence about the agreement.

**Security reports, Code of Conduct reports, and other requests:**

- your name or handle and contact details;
- the content of your report or request, including any information about other people that it contains; and
- our correspondence with you.

**Public contribution history:** your GitHub username, and the name and email address configured in your Git client, are recorded in commit history when your contribution is merged. Commit history is public by design.

Your information may be processed outside Nigeria, including in the United States (GitHub) and the European Union (CLA Assistant). Contact us for more information.

## How long we keep it

| Record                                                               | Retention                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| CLA acceptance records, signed corporate CLAs, and Schedule A        | For as long as any covered Contribution is distributed as part of the Project, plus six years. These records prove the licenses that allow the Project to be distributed |
| Correspondence about CLAs, trademark, licensing, or privacy requests | Three years after the matter is closed, unless needed for a legal claim                                                                                                  |
| Security reports                                                     | Three years after the related fix or advisory is published. Published advisories remain public                                                                           |
| Code of Conduct reports                                              | Three years after the matter is closed, unless needed for a legal claim or to enforce a ban                                                                              |
| Information provided to confirm your identity for a request          | Deleted when the request is completed                                                                                                                                    |

When records are no longer needed, we delete or anonymize them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where it applies, the GDPR or UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

- be informed about how your information is used;
- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- receive a portable copy of your information;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting earlier processing; and
- not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing.

To make a request, email **legal@richardkenneth.com**. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm your identity before fulfilling a request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

**Limits.** Some requests cannot be fully met:

- **CLA records:** we must keep a record of your acceptance for the retention period above, because it proves the licenses that let the Project be distributed. The licenses in the CLA are irrevocable.
- **Commit history:** your name, email, and username in merged commits are part of the Project's public version-control history and are copied into every clone and fork. We cannot remove them from copies held by others, and rewriting the history would affect every user of the Project. We will consider removing information from our own repository where the law requires it.
- **Corporate records:** where your employer signed a corporate CLA, we may need to keep records about you as an Authorized Contributor for the same reason.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority:

- in Nigeria, the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**, <https://ndpc.gov.ng>; or
- the data protection authority in the country where you live or work, or where the issue occurred.

## Security

We use access controls, private storage, multi-factor authentication on the accounts that hold these records, and encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us at **legal@richardkenneth.com**.

## Contributors under 18

The CLA requires contributors to be at least 18. If you are under 18, do not sign the CLA through the automated process. Contact us at **legal@richardkenneth.com** before contributing. We will accept a contribution only under a separate agreement countersigned by your parent or legal guardian, and we will collect only the information needed for that agreement.

We may update this notice as the project or its service providers change. The current version is published in this repository with its effective date. We will announce material changes in the repository before they take effect where practical.
