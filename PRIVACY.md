# Privacy Notice for CLA and Contributor Records

**Status:** pre-launch draft for review by qualified counsel.

This notice explains how SkillEx handles personal information collected when you sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA), contribute to the project, or request a corporate CLA. It does not cover the SkillEx product's end-user data; a separate product privacy policy will cover that.

## Who is responsible

The project owner is **Richard Kenneth**. For privacy requests, open a discussion at <https://github.com/richard-kenneth/skillex/discussions> and ask for a private channel. Do not post identity documents or sensitive personal information in a public discussion.

## What we collect

When you sign an individual CLA or contribute, we may collect:

- your GitHub username and GitHub user ID;
- your name and email address, if you choose to provide them or if they are available on your GitHub profile;
- the pull request number, repository, date, and CLA version associated with your acceptance;
- the custom fields you submit through the CLA service; and
- correspondence about your contribution or CLA.

When a corporation signs a corporate CLA, we may collect:

- the corporation's legal name, registered address, and country of incorporation;
- the authorized signatory's name, title, and business contact details;
- the names, GitHub usernames, email addresses, and affiliations of Authorized Contributors listed in Schedule A;
- the signed agreement and any updates to Schedule A; and
- correspondence about the agreement.

We do not ask you to post personal email addresses, signed agreements, or other sensitive information in public issues or discussions.

## Why we collect it

We use this information to:

- administer and record CLAs;
- verify that contributions are covered by the correct agreement;
- maintain records of contributions and licenses;
- respond to questions, requests, and legal claims;
- enforce the project's licenses and agreements; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## Legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on:

- **contract performance** for administering the CLA and the contributor relationship;
- **legitimate interests** in maintaining accurate records, protecting the project, and enforcing its licenses;
- **legal obligation** for records we must keep; and
- **consent** where we ask for optional information or where consent is required by law.

## Where the data is stored

- **Individual CLA signatures** are processed by the hosted CLA Assistant service operated by SAP. The CLA Assistant project states that signature data is stored in a Microsoft Azure Cosmos DB instance in Europe. See the [CLA Assistant Terms of Service and Privacy Policy](https://gist.github.com/CLAassistant/3a73e4cd729c9d0a6e30).
- **Corporate CLA documents and Schedule A** are stored in private project storage controlled by the Project Owner, not in the public repository.
- **Public repository:** we do not publish your personal email address or signed CLA documents in the repository. The repository may contain your GitHub username in contribution history, which is public by design.

## How long we keep it

We keep CLA records for as long as the project operates and for the period needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Corporate agreements are kept for the duration of the agreement and the applicable limitation period. When records are no longer needed, we delete or aggregate them.

## Who can access it

Access is limited to the Project Owner, maintainers who need it to administer contributions, legal counsel, and service providers that process it on our behalf (for example, the CLA Assistant and an e-signature or form provider). We do not sell personal information.

## International transfers

The CLA Assistant and other service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers. Contact us if you need more information.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- receive a portable copy of your information; and
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, open a discussion and ask for a private channel. We may need to verify your identity. Some requests may be limited by legal record-keeping requirements or by the irrevocable licenses in the CLA.

## Security

We use access controls, private storage, and encrypted connections to protect CLA records. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; if you believe your information has been compromised, contact us privately.

## Minors

If you are under the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction, the CLA requires parental or guardian consent. Contact us privately before contributing.

## Changes

We may update this notice as the project or its service providers change. The current version will be published in this repository. Material changes will be announced before they take effect where practical.

## Contact

Privacy questions or requests: open a discussion at <https://github.com/richard-kenneth/skillex/discussions> and ask for a private channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in the public post.
