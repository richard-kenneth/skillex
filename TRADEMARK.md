## What you may do without asking

You may use the SkillEx name and logo:

- to refer truthfully to the SkillEx project in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, and social posts;
- to link to the official repository, website, or documentation;
- to describe an unmodified copy of SkillEx that you have obtained from the official project, provided you do not imply sponsorship or endorsement; and
- in a factual statement such as "This project is a fork of SkillEx" or "Based on SkillEx".

When you use the name or logo, make it clear that you are referring to the project and not claiming to be the project, its maintainers, or an official representative.

## Forks and modified versions

The AGPL gives you the right to modify and distribute the code. It does not give you the right to use the SkillEx brand for your version.

If you distribute or publicly host a modified version, you must:

- use a different name and logo for your version;
- make it clear that your version is not the official SkillEx project; and
- keep any copyright, license, and attribution notices required by the AGPL.

You may still make a factual statement that your version is "forked from SkillEx" or "based on SkillEx", as long as it does not imply endorsement.
