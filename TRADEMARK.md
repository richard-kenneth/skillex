## What you may do without asking

You may use the SkillEx name, and the unmodified logo, to:

- refer truthfully to the SkillEx project in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, academic work, and social posts;
- link to the official repository, website, or documentation;
- state accurately that your software works with, integrates with, or is compatible with SkillEx; and
- make a factual statement such as "This project is a fork of SkillEx" or "Based on SkillEx".

When you use the name or logo, make it clear that you are referring to the project and not claiming to be the project, its maintainers, or an official representative.

## Forks, self-hosted copies, and modified versions

The AGPL gives you the right to run, modify, and distribute the code. It does not give you the right to use the SkillEx brand for your version or your service.

If you distribute a modified version, or operate a public deployment (modified or not), you must:

- use a different name and logo in the user-facing parts of your version or service, such as the product name, app name, page titles, emails, and app-store listings;
- make it clear that your version or service is not the official SkillEx project and is not operated by the Project Owner; and
- keep any copyright, license, and attribution notices required by the AGPL.

You may still state that your version is "forked from SkillEx" or "based on SkillEx", as long as you do not imply endorsement.

Private and internal deployments, such as a local development environment or an evaluation copy that is not offered to the public, do not need to be renamed.
